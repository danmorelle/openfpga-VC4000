Interton VC 4000 core for the Analogue Pocket, ported from the MiSTer VC4000 core. Also plays games for compatible consoles such as the Acetronic MPU-1000.

**Install:** unzip onto the root of your Pocket's SD card, then put your games (`.bin`) in `Assets/vc4000/common/`. No BIOS needed.

**Controls:** D-pad is the joystick, A is fire (keypad 2), B/X/Y are keypad 1/3/0, L/R are Clear/Enter, Start is Start and a tap of Select is Game Select. Hold Select for keypad 4 to 9 (X, Y, A, B, Up, Right).

**Settings:** Reset, Swap Controllers, D-Pad Mode (Absolute or Gradual) and Bright Colours.

Runs at the original 50 Hz PAL speed.
